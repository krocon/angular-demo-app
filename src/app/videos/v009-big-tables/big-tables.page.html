Grid frameworks virtualize both directions

CDK virtual scroll only virtualizes rows, needs a fixed row height and leaves sorting and filtering to you. Grid frameworks virtualize rows and columns:

  • ag-Grid – feature-rich, community + enterprise editions.
  • GUI Expert Table – MIT, renders tables with more than 1 million rows; used above as the 4th mode.

Full disclosure: GUI Expert is the author's project.
